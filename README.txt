GIFT PLANNER - host these files together in one folder over https, then open the address in Safari on your iPhone.
Share > Add to Home Screen, then open it from the Home Screen.
Adding people: Contacts > Share Contact > Save to Files, then People > Import from Contacts.
Add from Contacts: People > Add from Contacts (needs a one-time Shortcut, steps are in the app).
Moving your old data: in the old Claude copy go to People > Backup & restore and copy the text.
In the new Home Screen app go to People > Backup & restore, paste, and tap Restore.
